```release-note:breaking-change
resource/k0rdent_security_group: A group with no `ingress` and no `egress` block fails the plan with "A security group needs at least one rule", because the platform refuses a group with no rules. Add an `ingress` or `egress` block to keep the group. To delete it, remove it from what it's bound to, then remove its resource block and apply. `terraform destroy` fails the same way while the block has no rule
```
