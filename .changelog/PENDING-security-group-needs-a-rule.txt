```release-note:breaking-change
resource/k0rdent_security_group: A plan to create a group with no `ingress` and no `egress` block, or to change a group that has none, fails with "A security group needs at least one rule", because the platform refuses either. Add an `ingress` or `egress` block. A group already in state with no rules and nothing to change plans with a warning, and `terraform destroy` deletes it. A tainted group with no rules, or one whose configuration also changes it, stops `terraform destroy` with the same error: remove its block and apply, or run `terraform destroy -refresh=false`
```
