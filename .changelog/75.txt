```release-note:bug
resource/k0rdent_file_store: Fix a create whose wait timed out, was interrupted or failed leaving no `uid` in state for it, so the next refresh took whatever object it found under the name. After the object was deleted outside Terraform and another made with the same name, `terraform untaint` then `terraform destroy` deleted the other one. The create now records the `uid` from its answer, or from the read that claims the object, before it waits
```
