```release-note:note
data-source/k0rdent_tenant_quota: Deprecated. Use `k0rdent_organization_quota` instead, which names the organization's scope `organization`. `k0rdent_tenant_quota` is removed in v1.0.0, and reports `tenant` until then on a platform either side of the rename
```

```release-note:breaking-change
data-source/k0rdent_project_quota: `binding_scope` and `scopes[*].scope` name the organization's scope `organization` rather than `tenant`, whichever the platform sends. Compare with `organization`
```

```release-note:enhancement
provider: The quota lines the provider adds to a `QUOTA_EXCEEDED` refusal name the organization's scope `organization` whichever the platform sends. The platform's own message is shown as sent, so a platform from before the rename still says `tenant` there
```
